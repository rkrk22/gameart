What is the principle behind "changing" recurring objects?

Let's recap the lesson one more time. We have repeated identical elements. That's not a bad thing, but..
https://ik.imagekit.io/71jz3gcgav/image_cGPt9MYBH.png

But if we want to give a rhythm, we can change the size one of them:

https://ik.imagekit.io/71jz3gcgav/image_O-wYjxboj.png

If we continue by copying the whole row, we get the same repetition again:
https://ik.imagekit.io/71jz3gcgav/image_DvcjnOLg-.png

Now for this large array, we need to make one more change

For example, let's shrink one cube:
https://ik.imagekit.io/71jz3gcgav/image_R2-hFCdzq.png

And we get a rhythm of identical objects. Which is diluted by other figures, also identical but having their own rhythm. To put it simply, they will also repeat themselves, but with a slight change:
https://ik.imagekit.io/71jz3gcgav/image_P7hVO1kRw.png

This rhythm of cubes can remind us of music. Think about the way the notes are arranged. They follow each other in a certain order interrupted by other "accents". And it all comes together to create a larger composition.
