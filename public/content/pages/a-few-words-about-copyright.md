## Before You Start

This guide was created to share the character development process, from the first decisions to the finished result. Its purpose is to help you understand the principles behind the workflow and apply them to creating your own characters.

All original materials presented here, including the character design, sketches, models, textures, and other development work, were created by Ruslan Kim for the game **Croak The Swamp** and are protected by copyright.

You may study these materials and follow the exercises in this guide for personal learning purposes. Access to the guide does not grant permission to use the featured character or any of its elements in your own games, in commercial projects, for sale, or for distribution. Giving credit to the author does not, by itself, replace that permission.

If you recreate the character step by step while learning, the result remains a study copy. Do not present it as your own original design. You must obtain the author’s permission in advance before publishing copies or using the materials beyond personal learning.

**Study the techniques, try different solutions, and use what you learn to create something of your own.** This is what will help you develop your skills and find your own approach.

As you continue through the guide, please keep these terms in mind and respect the author’s work.

Thank you for your understanding, and enjoy learning!
