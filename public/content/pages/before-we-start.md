# Before we start

I have my own project—an indie game about musician frogs. Each character has their own personality and skills. We explore a swamp-themed location, complete tasks, and most importantly, put together a band to perform on the main stage. But the success of the performance will depend on which instruments you’ve collected, who you’ve invited to join your band, and how well you handle the rhythm gameplay. And all of this takes place in a cozy atmosphere with funny dialogues.
